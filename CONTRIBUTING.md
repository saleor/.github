# Contributing

We value your contributions to Saleor and want to ensure they meet our project's needs.
To help us maintain quality and consistency, we ask that you follow the process described
in our [Contribution Guidelines]. We welcome issues, new features, documentation
improvements, community support, and more.

## Before opening a pull request

- **Discuss your proposed change with maintainers before starting work.**
  Open an issue or GitHub discussion to explain the problem, its impact on users,
  and your proposed solution. This helps establish whether the change is necessary,
  desirable, and aligned with the project's priorities.
- **Do not assume that an open issue is an invitation to submit a fix.** Existing issues,
  including those reported months or years ago, may no longer be relevant,
  may have been deprioritized, or may not align with the project's current roadmap.
  Only propose fixes for issues that directly affect your use, deployment, or maintenance
  of Saleor.
- **Consider the long-term maintenance burden.** Even technically correct changes
  introduce review, testing, and ongoing maintenance costs. Contributions should
  provide meaningful value that justifies these costs.
- Read the general [Contribution Guidelines].

Maintainers may decline proposed changes before or after implementation, including fixes
for legitimate bugs, when they do not align with the project's priorities or when their
expected benefits do not justify the review and maintenance burden.

## Generative AI policy

**Generative AI is permitted as a development aid or tool.** Contributors are welcome
to use AI for research, understanding code, debugging, writing code, and testing.
AI-assisted or AI-generated code is acceptable, provided that contributors personally
review, understand, and validate the resulting changes and take full responsibility
for their work.

However, the following restrictions apply to ensure that AI remains an aid rather
than a replacement for human contributors:

- **No autonomous contributions.** AI tools or agents must not autonomously create,
  open, submit, or manage pull requests, issues, or other contributions.
- **No AI-generated communications.** Contributors must personally write and
  communicate with maintainers. AI tools or agents must not generate pull request
  descriptions, issues, comments, review replies, or other communications directed
  at maintainers, whether autonomously or under human supervision.
  Maintainers expect to interact with contributors directly, not their chatbots.
- **Human understanding and accountability.** Contributors must personally review,
  understand, and validate any AI-assisted changes before submission.
  They must be able to explain their changes, justify their necessity, and address
  maintainer feedback without relying on an AI agent to communicate on their behalf.

**Generative AI is a development aid**, not a substitute for contributor involvement,
judgment, or responsibility. All contributions, whether AI-assisted or not, must
comply with the project's contribution guidelines.

Using AI to assist with development is acceptable. Using AI as a substitute for
human judgment, communication, or accountability is not. All contributions,
whether AI-assisted or not, must comply with the project's contribution guidelines

Maintainers reserve the right to reject or close contributions, including without further
review, when they suspect violations of this policy, insufficient human oversight, or
low-effort or low-quality submissions.

Repeated or serious violations may result in temporary suspension or a permanent ban
from future contributions, at the maintainers' discretion.

## Bulk and automated submissions

Contributors must not submit pull requests, issues, or other contributions in bulk,
whether manually or through bots, scripts, or other automated tools.

Contributions must be individually assessed for relevance, necessity, and value
to the project before submission. Maintainers may reject contributions, including
without further review, based on submission volume, repetitive or low-value changes,
or concerns about the review burden imposed on the project.

Systematically working through the issue tracker to submit fixes, particularly for
old or low-priority issues, without prior maintainer agreement is prohibited.
An open issue does not establish that fixing it is a current project priority.

Splitting bulk changes across multiple accounts, repositories, or time periods to
circumvent this policy is prohibited.

Violations may result in rejection or closure of submissions without further review.
Repeated or serious violations may result in temporary suspension or a permanent ban
from future contributions, at the maintainers' discretion.

## Contributor eligibility

We only accept contributions from individuals or organizations that actively use,
deploy, or maintain the project.

This requirement ensures that contributions remain aligned with the project's priorities,
objectives, and actual user needs, while allowing maintainers to allocate their limited
review capacity to changes that deliver meaningful value to the project and its community.

Contributors may be asked to explain their use of the project, how the reported problem
directly affects their use, deployment, or maintenance of Saleor, and how the proposed
contribution addresses that problem.

Maintainers reserve the right to reject or close contributions, including without further
review, when their relevance, practical value, or alignment with project priorities cannot
be established. The existence of an open issue does not automatically imply that external
contributions addressing it will be accepted.
